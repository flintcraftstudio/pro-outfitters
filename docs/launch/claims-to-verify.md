# Pro Outfitters: claims to verify before launch

Compiled 2026-10-28 from the text of every public page on the current build.
Each item quotes what the site says and where. "Request #" points to the
matching item in [client-request-list.md](client-request-list.md).

Goal: nothing on the site a guest could hold up and say "but your website
said…". Where a claim can't be confirmed, soften it or cut it.

---

## 1. Contradictions: the site disagrees with itself

These must be settled before launch: two pages can't both be right.

1. **How long Pro has been guiding.** (Request #2)
   - Home: "We've spent **fifty years** earning access to water most people never see."
   - About, footer, home hero: "**since 1970**" / "EST. 1970" (56 years).
   - North Fork, Sharptail, Smith River: "**close to forty years**."
   - Ask: which number does Brandon stand behind, and what does it count
     from (the company's founding, or his and the current crew's time on
     the water)? Both can be true if they measure different things.

2. **Sharptail acreage.** (Request #5)
   - Sharptail lodge card, eyebrow and intro: "**150,000+** huntable acres."
   - The old site also said "over **200,000** acres of leased ground."
   - Ask: which figure, and is it huntable acres or leased acres?

3. **Yurt at Craig capacity.**
   - Detail block: "Sleeps **up to 6**."
   - Body copy: main yurt with two bedrooms (one queen each) plus two
     detached guest bedrooms that "sleep two apiece": that adds up to **8**.
   - Ask: what is the real maximum, and how is it laid out?

4. **North Fork bathrooms.**
   - North Fork page: tent cabins with "a **private bathhouse**."
   - Fred G.'s full Google review (not the excerpt on the site): "The tent
     rooms where very nice and you have **your own bathroom**."
   - Ask: does each tent cabin have its own bathroom, or share a bathhouse?

5. **Fishing seasons.**
   - North Fork Crossing: season "**June through mid-October**."
   - Blackfoot River and Fly Fishing pages: day floats "**April through
     October**" (from the old Blackfoot page).
   - Both can be right (lodge season vs. day-float season); confirm both.

## 2. Facts that may be out of date

6. **Brandon's bio.** (Request #8) About: "A guide for **27 years** and the
   owner of Pro Outfitters for the last **20**." This dates from 2021. Ask
   for the year he started guiding and the year he took over, so the page
   can say "since" and never go stale.

7. **Guide and chef bios.** (Request #9, #10) About:
   - Joel Loran, "Guiding for 27 years."
   - Pat Kane, "Guiding for 25 years."
   - Michael Carlucci, "has cooked for Pro Outfitters since 2000."
   - Keith Kelly, Chef, North Fork Crossing Lodge.
   - Elli Ortloff, Office Manager.
   - Guest reviews name people who aren't on the page: Jared, Elijah (Kids
     Camp counselor), Graham (Smith River chef), Pride, Shannon, Dawson.
   - Ask: who is on staff for 2026, with current titles and start years?

8. **Smith River booking.** (Request #3)
   - Smith River page: "That **permit lottery** is exactly why the fishing
     stays low-pressure" and "books far in advance **through the state
     lottery**."
   - As I understand it, guided trips run on outfitter launch allocations
     rather than the public lottery; if so, telling guests they need to win
     a lottery is wrong and could cost bookings.
   - Ask: how does a guest actually get on a Smith trip with Pro?

9. **Smith River length.** Smith River page: "a **sixty-one-mile**
   wilderness float." Several sources give 59 miles (Camp Baker to Eden
   Bridge). Ask which figure Pro uses. Also confirm "a **thousand-foot**
   limestone canyon."

10. **Kids Camp details.** (Request #16)
    - "**One week** each summer" and "two campers to every guide": from the
      old site, which listed 2023 dates (July 23–29).
    - Also needed: ages, and whether parents stay at the lodge.

11. **Bird hunting packages.** Bird Hunting page: "Packages run **three or
    four days**, and a four-day package can include a day fishing the
    Missouri or one of the small freestone creeks nearby." Source: a 2020
    blog post. Still offered?

12. **Driving directions and addresses.** (Request #19) Transcribed from the
    old site's 2021 directions sheets, now on the North Fork and Sharptail
    pages: 328 North Fork Hill Road, Ovando, MT 59854 and 445 Sharptail
    Lane, Geyser, MT 59447. Ask Brandon to read both routes through once,
    and confirm he's comfortable publishing the Sharptail address.

## 3. Endorsements, partnerships and credentials

Claims a third party could check. Each needs a yes before launch.

13. **Orvis endorsements.** (Request #6)
    - Home hero: "EST. 1970 · **ORVIS-ENDORSED** · MONTANA."
    - North Fork: "Orvis Endorsed Fly Fishing Lodge · Orvis Endorsed Kids Camp."
    - Sharptail: "Orvis Endorsed Wingshooting Lodge."
    - Footer: "**Orvis-endorsed since the rivers were quieter**" and
      "Orvis-Endorsed Outfitter."
    - Ask: all three current for 2026? Is "Orvis-Endorsed Outfitter" (the
      company, not a lodge) accurate? The footer line implies a long
      endorsement history; keep it only if that's true.

14. **Trout Unlimited.** (Request #7)
    - Home: "We **partner with Trout Unlimited** on habitat and cold-water
      restoration across the watersheds we guide."
    - Home chips: "Trout Unlimited partner," "Catch & release waters,"
      "Limited daily access." Footer and contact: "Trout Unlimited Partner."
    - The old site showed the "Official member of Trout Unlimited's
      Outfitters, Guides & Business Members Program" banner: membership,
      which is narrower than a habitat partnership.
    - Ask: what is the actual relationship? Which rivers are catch and
      release? Does Pro limit its days, and how?

15. **Outfitter license.** Contact page: "Brandon Boedecker, **Montana
    Outfitter #6481**." Confirm the number is current.

16. **Forest Service permit.** (Request #4) Smith River page, contact page:
    "Smith River trips operated under **special-use permit with the
    Helena–Lewis and Clark National Forest**." Confirm the forest name
    matches the permit.

## 4. Descriptive claims worth a quick yes/no

17. **Home:** "Wade and float the Missouri, the Blackfoot, and **tailwaters
    that fish all season**." The Missouri below Holter is a tailwater; the
    Blackfoot is not. Which tailwaters does Pro guide?
18. **Home and About:** "**Owner-operated**" / "Every Pro lodge is
    owner-operated." True of all three, including The Yurt at Craig?
19. **About:** "**Chef-prepared meals**" as a general promise. The Yurt at
    Craig is self-catered; the About copy should say where meals are
    included.
20. **North Fork:** "**five artesian-fed ponds** — stocked"; "custom feather
    beds, heat, electricity."
21. **Sharptail:** "homesteaded in the **late 1800s**"; "the **original red
    barn** now houses our dogs"; yurts with "two queen beds, private baths,
    and gas fireplaces"; bar stocked with beer, wine and mixers, guests
    bring their own liquor.
22. **Sharptail:** "**some of the most prolific wild bird habitat in
    America**." A superlative: keep only if Brandon can stand behind it.
23. **Yurt at Craig:** "**Some of the finest trout water in the world** runs
    past the front door"; "eight minutes from the Wolf Creek take-out,
    nineteen from Holter Lake."
24. **Smith River:** "**the only river in Montana** where every recreational
    float is regulated by limited permit."
25. **Bird hunting:** pheasant season opens "after about the **seventh of
    October**"; season "**September 1 through mid-November**." These
    follow the state seasons, which change; confirm for 2026.
26. **Packing lists** (carried over from the old site): beer and wine
    provided on the Smith; a sleeping bag provided on request; all fishing
    gear provided at no additional cost; North Fork provides towels,
    toiletries, hairdryers and bathrobes; shot sizes for the hunt. (Request
    #17)

## 6. Not for the client: site fixes on our side

- **Site name.** Page titles and the footer copyright come from the
  `APP_NAME` env var. Locally they read "Standard Template"; staging reads
  "pro-outfitters". Production must set `APP_NAME="Pro Outfitters"`.
- **Fixed already:**
  - The Fly Fishing page said every lodge has "meals cooked on site"; The
    Yurt at Craig is self-catered, so that line is gone.
  - The footer copyright read "© 1970–2026"; it is now "© <year> Pro
    Outfitters". "Est. 1970" stays in the wordmark.
  - The footer's "Rates & Seasons" link went to `/lodges`, which shows no
    rates. It's gone; add it back if the client wants rates published
    online.
