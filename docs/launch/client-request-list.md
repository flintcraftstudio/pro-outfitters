## The request list

### 1. Facts to confirm

1. **Company history.** The homepage says the business is still run by the family that started it in 1970. Is that right, or did you buy it? I'll write it whichever way is true.
2. **Years guiding.** Some pages say "fifty years," others say "close to forty years." Which number do you want to stand behind, and what does it count from?
3. **Smith River booking.** How does a guest actually get on a Smith trip with you? Does it run through the state permit lottery, or do you hold the permits as an outfitter?
4. **Forest name.** Does your special-use permit say "Helena–Lewis and Clark National Forest"?
5. **Sharptail acreage.** Your old site says both "150,000 huntable acres" and "200,000+ acres of leased ground." Which should we lead with?
6. **Orvis endorsements.** Are all three current for 2026: Fly Fishing Lodge (North Fork), Kids Camp, and Wingshooting Lodge (Sharptail)?
7. **Stewardship.** The homepage mentions a Trout Unlimited partnership on habitat work, limiting the number of days you run, and catch-and-release waters. Which of those are true and fair to say?
8. **Your bio.** "A guide for 27 years, owner for 20" is from 2021. What year did you start guiding, and what year did you take over? I'll write it as "since" so it never goes stale.

### 2. People

9. **Staff roster.** Are Elli, Joel, Pat, Michael, and Keith all still with you, with the same titles? Anyone to add?
10. **Guiding years.** For each guide, the year they started, for the same reason as your bio.
11. **Headshots.** The current photos are small and will look soft. Larger originals or new photos would help. A phone photo in good light works.

### 3. Guest quotes

12. **Testimonials.** Three to five real quotes from guests, with how they'd like to be credited (full name, first name and state, or initials). The homepage quote is a placeholder and comes down before launch. Good sources are emails, cards, or reviews you've received. Please confirm each guest is fine being quoted.

### 4. Rates and booking

13. **Rates.** Should the site publish prices, show "starting at," or keep it to "inquire for rates"? There's a "Rates & Seasons" link that needs somewhere honest to go.
14. **Policies.** Your deposit and cancellation terms, if you want them on the site.
15. **Common questions.** The five questions the office answers most often. That's the start of a short FAQ.

### 5. Pages your current site has

16. **Kids Camp.** Ages, a typical day, dates and session length, whether parents stay, and any photos.
17. **Packing lists.** Are the Smith River, Kids Camp, and bird hunting lists on your current site still accurate? Mark up anything that's changed.
18. **North Fork trip details.** Is that page still current? Where do guests meet, and how do they get there?
19. **Directions.** The physical address or meeting point for each lodge, so guests can find it on a map.
20. **Blackfoot River.** Keep it as its own page, or fold it into North Fork Crossing?
21. **News, videos, and blog posts.** Is anything there worth keeping? Anything we drop will still send visitors to the right place on the new site.
22. **Sharptail video.** Should the YouTube film go on the Sharptail page?

### 6. Photos

23. **Original photos.** The current photos are web-sized from 2014–2021 and look soft on large screens. Do you or your photographer have the full-size originals?
24. **A new shoot.** If the originals are gone, is a shoot something you'd like to plan? It doesn't need to block launch. We can swap photos in afterward.
25. **Logos.** Orvis Endorsed and Trout Unlimited logo files, if you have them, and confirmation you're allowed to display them.

### 7. Accounts and access

26. **Domain.** Who your domain is registered with, and access to its DNS settings (or the person who manages it).
27. **Email.** Who hosts your email (Google, Microsoft, your web host?). I need to switch the website without touching your email.
28. **Inquiry inbox.** Which email address or addresses should new trip inquiries go to?
29. **Admin logins.** Who should be able to log in to read inquiries? I need their email addresses.
30. **Analytics.** Do you have an existing Google Analytics account, Facebook/Meta business account, or Google Business Profile? Access to any of them.
31. **Facebook page.** Confirm the link is facebook.com/prooutfitters.
