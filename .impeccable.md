## Design Context

### Users
Affluent sporting travelers booking $3k–$10k+ guided Montana trips: fly fishing floats (Smith, Missouri, Blackfoot), upland wingshooting at Sharptail, and lodge stays. Discerning, often older, value authenticity, conservation ethic, and earned access over flash. Many are returning guests. They browse to decide whether Pro Outfitters is the right outfit, then **convert mostly by phoning the office** (Elli); the inquiry form is secondary. Pages must make the phone number easy to find and tap, and treat the form as the quieter alternative.

### Brand Personality
Confident, understated, place-rooted. A well-read river guide, not a brochure. Three words: **calm, earned, unhurried.** The first ten seconds should land as **calm trust**: "these people have done this for fifty years; I'm in good hands." Restraint over excitement. No superlatives, adventure-brochure language, exclamation marks, or emoji.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial-minimal, in the register of luxury hospitality (Aman, Relais & Châteaux, The Ranch at Rock Creek): photography carries the emotion, layout stays calm and spacious. Warm paper-and-ink near-monochrome (bone #FAF7F1, ink #1A1815), one muted slate accent (#3F6175) for links/focus, heritage antique gold (#A58F58) only as fine rules and eyebrow accents. Newsreader display serif + Libre Franklin body; letter-spaced uppercase eyebrows; the gold hairline rule as signature ornament. Light theme only. Motion is slow and sparing ("lazy river"), always respecting reduced motion.

**Anti-references:** rugged-lodge clichés (faux wood, leather, antlers, rope borders), adventure/outdoor-retail energy (bold sans, high-saturation, "epic"), generic AI templates (identical card grids, hero-metric stat bands, centered-everything).

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint is the brand.** Remove before adding; whitespace and type do the work.
2. **Photography leads, layout serves.** Choose and crop images deliberately; never let UI fight the photo.
3. **Make calling effortless.** The phone is the primary conversion: visible, tappable, and named as a real person/office, with the form as a calm secondary path.
4. **Earned, specific claims only.** Every fact verifiable; specificity (rivers, species, seasons, names) over adjectives.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA contrast (including text over photos), visible focus, reduced-motion respected, comfortable reading sizes for an older audience.
